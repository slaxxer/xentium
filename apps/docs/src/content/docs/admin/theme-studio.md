Theme Studio is where you arrange your pages and style your theme. You find it at
**ACP → Appearance → Theme Studio**. Everything you do there is a draft until you press
**Publish**, so you can try things without your members seeing them.

Each theme, and the built-in Core theme, keeps its own layouts and style. Pick the theme you
want to edit at the top of the page, and the page you want to arrange in the page picker:
the home page, articles, profiles, members, or the site's header and footer, which show on
every page.

## The page is a stack of sections

That's the whole idea: a page is built from **sections**, a section has **columns**, and a
column holds **blocks**.

On the left, **Add** offers ready-made sections first:

- **Hero**: a big heading, a line of text and a button.
- **Three features**: three columns, each with a short heading and text.
- **Text**: a heading and a paragraph.
- **Call to action**: a coloured band with a heading and a button.
- **Empty, one column** and **Empty, two columns**: room for your own blocks.

Drag one onto the page, between two sections, or click it to add it after the section you
selected. Below the sections is the block library: articles, members, stats, and the blocks
your add-ons bring, each named with its add-on.

Select a section to change its **Columns**: one, two (even, wide left or wide right) or three.
Fewer columns never remove a block. What doesn't fit moves into the last column.

**Outline**, the other tab on the left, lists every section, column and block on the page. It
also shows blocks the current preview doesn't draw, like the ones only guests see, so nothing
can hide from you.

## Moving things

Drag a block and every place it can go lights up as a faint line, with the nearest one shown
solid. You see where it will land before you let go. A section moves between sections, a block
moves into a column. A spot that can't take the block doesn't light up.

You can also move a block with the arrows in its settings, or with **Move to**.

## Typing on the page

Double-click a heading or the hero text and type over it. Press Enter or click elsewhere to keep
it, Escape to cancel.

Most of the words on a fresh site are translated: a visitor reading in German sees them in
German. When you type over such a text, it becomes your own text, the same in every language,
and the block's settings say so. **Use the translated text again** brings the translated
version back.

## Settings

The pane on the right shows the selected block's settings. With nothing selected, it shows the
theme's style: its colourways, corners and glow, with a link to every colour and font. Click
something on the page to change just that.

**Simple** shows the settings most people need. **Advanced** adds who sees a block, edge art,
style variants and CSS handles.

- **Who sees it**: everyone, guests only, members only, or chosen groups. This changes what a
  page shows. It doesn't protect anything: a hidden block's content is still reachable where it
  normally lives.
- **Edge art**: a shape along the top or bottom edge of a block that paints a surface: a
  section, the header, the navbar, the footer, and the cards (the home page cards, the profile
  panels, the members page). If your theme cuts the corners of cards, that cut also hides
  anything outside the card, so a new edge on a card starts **Inward**. Choose **Outward** and
  the panel tells you why it may not show.
- Add-ons can put their own settings on the blocks they belong to. An add-on that rings
  avatars, for example, adds its setting to the profile's avatar block. What it sets is part of
  the theme's style and shows on every page once you publish.

## Seeing what changed

The **changes** button at the top counts what publishing would change. Turn it on, and every new,
edited or moved block is outlined on the page with a **Changed** tag. Blocks you removed since
the last publish are listed there too, with **Put back**.

## Previewing

At the top of the preview:

- **Viewing as** Guests or Members: the page as each of them sees it.
- Desktop, tablet or phone width. On a narrower screen the preview zooms to fit.

Undo and redo sit beside the page picker, and work with Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z too.

## Publishing

**Publish** puts every draft live at once: the layouts of every page and the theme's style.
Members' open tabs update within seconds, without reloading.

**History** keeps what you published before, so you can go back to an earlier version.

**Reset** puts one page back to its built-in arrangement. It can't be undone.

Some blocks can't be deleted, only moved: the site logo and the account menu in the navbar. A
site without them would lock people out.
